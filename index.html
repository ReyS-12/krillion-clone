How to play

Give one answer before the clock runs out. The first recognized answer locks in. You can retry an unrecognized guess while time remains.

  • Common · 1–30
  • Uncommon · 31–60
  • Rare · 61–89
  • Deep Cut · 90–99
  • One in a Krillion · 100, plus a fact

Each answer has a fixed rarity score. In Infinity Mode, score at least 40 points to keep your oxygen. The target rises by 5 every five rounds, up to 80. A timeout scores zero.
